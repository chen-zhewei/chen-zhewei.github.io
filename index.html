关于我 About Me

八岁那年，我第一次真正触摸到计算机的世界。没有老师，没有课程，只有拆了又装、坏了再修的好奇心。从 HTML 到 Python，从 Minecraft 服务器到树莓派硬件，十余年自驱探索从未间断。我用 Git 管理每一次提交，在 GitHub 上记录成长轨迹。

如今，我熟练运用 Python 开发检索式问答 AI，独立运维 Paper 插件服务器，也在树莓派、ESP8266 与 BadUSB 之间寻找硬件的乐趣。偶尔涉猎网络渗透，持续在算法、硬件与游戏之间构建属于自己的数字世界。

At age eight, I first truly touched the world of computers. No teachers, no courses—just a curiosity to take things apart and rebuild them. From HTML to Python, from Minecraft servers to Raspberry Pi hardware, over a decade of uninterrupted self-driven exploration. I use Git for every commit, tracing my growth on GitHub.

Today, I develop retrieval-based Q&A AI with Python, independently operate a Paper plugin server, and explore hardware through Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, and BadUSB. Occasionally dabbling in network penetration, I continue to build my own digital world across algorithms, hardware, and games.
